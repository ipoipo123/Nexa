About NexaPay

A front-end e-wallet prototype designed for a school performance task. It demonstrates account creation, multiple users, bank transfers, mobile load and bill payment simulations, savings goals, a paper portfolio, balance management, transaction records, PIN confirmation, QR receiving and printable receipts.

Terms & conditions

Plain-language terms for this educational wallet prototype.

i
Demo use only

NexaPay is a school-project simulation. It is not a bank, payment service or stored-value facility.

01

No real money or payments

Balances, top-ups, wallet and bank transfers, withdrawals, mobile load, bill payments and receipts are simulated for demonstration. No funds are deposited, held, moved or redeemable for cash, and no telco, biller or bank is contacted.

02

Local demo data

Account details and activity are saved in this browser. Anyone with access to this device or browser profile may be able to view or change that data.

03

Use demo credentials

Do not enter real passwords, wallet PINs, bank details or other sensitive personal information. The prototype does not provide production-grade account security.

04

Illustrative limits

Displayed transfer and withdrawal limits are example rules enforced only inside this demo. They are not the limits or policies of GCash, Maya or any financial institution.

05

Prototype availability

Features may change or stop working as the project is updated. Keep anything you need outside the demo; browser data can be cleared at any time.

06

Grow is simulated

Savings goals and the paper portfolio are local demonstrations only. They do not provide a savings account, investment, insurance, interest, market prices, coverage or returns. Paper positions stay at their contributed value.

QUICK HELP

Using the demo wallet

What is Grow?

Use savings goals to set aside demo wallet funds or create paper portfolio positions. Contributions and withdrawals ask for your PIN, and positions can be closed at their contributed amount.

How do I receive money?

Open My QR and share your QR code or NexaPay username with another demo user.

How do load, bills and bank transfers work?

Choose an option, review it and confirm with your PIN. These actions only record simulated wallet transactions; they do not contact external services.

Why does an action ask for my PIN?

The PIN confirms simulated top-ups, transfers, withdrawals, load purchases, bill payments, bank transfers and Grow movements before they are recorded.

Where is my account data?

It is stored locally in this browser. This prototype does not connect to a real financial account.
